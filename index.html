Lesson 1.2.6 Overview

Personal Information

Total Duration

60 minutes

Learn to give, request, record and confirm personal information clearly. Practice listening to fictional customers and handling everyday and workplace registration conversations accurately.

01. Provide

Say and spell names, ages, phone numbers, addresses and emails.

02. Clarify

Ask for repetition, spelling and confirmation politely.

03. Verify

Listen, record and check information accurately.

Encounter · Spot the Information

10 min

Study this fictional registration form. What information is included? Why must it be recorded correctly?

Sample registration form

Full name
Taylor Brooks
Age
30
Phone
(202) 555-0146
Email
t.brooks@example.com
Address
24 Oak Street, Austin, TX 78701

Discuss with a partner

  1. Which details are numbers? Which require spelling?
  2. What could happen if a phone number or email is incorrect?
  3. What would you ask if you did not hear the street name?
Privacy reminder: All profiles in this lesson are invented. Do not enter or share your own personal contact details.

Clarify · Say It Clearly

15 min

Follow your trainer's examples. Listen, repeat, and practice the information with a partner. Your trainer will model pronunciation and help you check your answers.

Age: 13 vs. 30

thirteen / thirty

Trainer tip: Practice the stress difference, then say “I'm thirteen years old” and “I'm thirty years old.”

Phone number

202-555-0146

Read digits individually: two zero two, five five five, zero one four six.

Email symbols

@ = at · . = dot · _ = underscore · - = hyphen

Practice: alex_lee@example.com → “alex underscore lee at example dot com.”

Address

24 Oak Street, Austin, Texas 78701

Say the street number, street name, city, state, and ZIP code clearly.

Useful clarification phrases

“Could you spell your last name, please?”
“Was that thirteen or thirty?”
“Could you repeat the last four digits?”
“Let me confirm your email address.”
“Is that correct?”
“B as in Bravo.”
Trainer-led practice: Listen to your trainer read a fictional phone number, email, or address. Write it down, compare with a partner, and ask for clarification when necessary. Switch roles for a second round.
